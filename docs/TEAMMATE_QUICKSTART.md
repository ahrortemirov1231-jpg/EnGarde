# Getting into the team workspace (5 minutes, once)

The app now runs on one computer and you open it in your browser. You do not install the app.

1. Install **Tailscale** from https://tailscale.com/download . It is a small free app that connects you
   privately to the team's computer. Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android all work.
2. Sign in to Tailscale with a **personal** Google, Microsoft, GitHub or Apple account. Do not use your NYU email.
3. Open the **share link** Ahror sent you and choose **Accept**.
4. Open the **team link** Ahror sent you (it ends in `.ts.net`) and sign in with the name and first password
   he gave you. Bookmark the page.
5. Open the **Team** page and change your password.

Every time after that: check that Tailscale is on, then open the bookmark.

Good to know
- If the page says "This site can't be reached", the team computer is off or asleep. Nothing is lost. Ask in the group chat.
- Everything you label is saved under your name, straight away, for everyone.
- The **Home** page shows the version and what changed. The (i) button above the touch list explains the words under each touch.
- A locked video cannot be changed. Use **Ask to unlock** on its card and say what needs changing.
- Please do not download or pass on the videos. Some fencers in them are under 14.
